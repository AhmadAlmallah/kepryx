# Decision - open-source product

Kepryx will launch as an open-source product/community preview under the author's identity,
with Apache-2.0 code and maintainer-led governance. A dedicated organization is preferred when
available, while authorship remains explicit.

The immediate goal is reputation, technical evidence, and design-partner feedback. Paid services
or hosted operations are deferred until repeated external demand proves a real commercial wedge.
